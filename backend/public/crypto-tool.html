Password Encrypt / Decrypt

Client-side AES-256-GCM — nothing is sent to the server
Encrypt
Decrypt
Uses PBKDF2 (150,000 iterations, SHA-256) to derive a key from your passphrase,
then AES-256-GCM to encrypt. Salt + IV are embedded in the output, base64-encoded.
The same passphrase is required to decrypt. Lose it and the data is unrecoverable.
