# Regulatory source register

Research date: **2026-10-13**. Retrieved: 2026-10-13 for every row.

How to read this register
- **Confidence High** — the obligation was confirmed on an official (government, regulator, board) page or PDF in this session.
- **Medium** — confirmed from an official document reproduced by an institution (e.g. a CBSE letter a school posted), or an official page that was found but not opened in full.
- **Low / UNVERIFIED** — from secondary reporting (news, law blogs) or from prior knowledge not re-confirmed in this session. Such rows are configured as *UNDER_REVIEW* in the ERP and must be confirmed by a qualified person before the institution treats them as binding.
- Nothing here is legal advice. The ERP records applicability decisions made by the institution; it does not make them.

Limitations of this session: four research subagents launched for this work stopped on a rate limit before producing output, so the research was redone directly with targeted searches. Several official PDFs (indiacode.nic.in, pib.gov.in, labour.gov.in) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching; where only the search-result extract of an official document was available, the row says so.
